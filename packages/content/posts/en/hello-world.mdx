## Why a test post

A blog is only as good as its worst-rendered element. This page uses the basics, so a change to the typography shows up here first. Read more on the [Astro documentation](https://docs.astro.build) and keep `pnpm check` green.

### What it covers

The post exercises the following elements:

- headings of two levels
- a paragraph with a link and `inline code`
- an unordered list
- a blockquote
- a fenced code block

> Think beyond limits.
